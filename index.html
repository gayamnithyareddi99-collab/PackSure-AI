FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PackSure AI and what it means for your brand's packaging quality, regulatory compliance, and operational efficiency.

What is PackSure AI and why do UK brands need it?

+
PackSure AI is an AI-powered sustainable packaging and product authentication platform designed to bridge the "Packaging and Trust Execution Gap" for UK SMEs, importers, and consumer brands. By acting as a sophisticated "packaging DNA" layer, the platform synthesises raw product data weight, fragility, and storage requirements into optimised, compliant packaging designs. Without it, UK brands face £2.5 billion in annual losses from transit damage, refusals, and regulatory non-compliance.

What is Packaging DNA Fingerprinting?

+
Packaging DNA Fingerprinting uses machine learning to identify and "fingerprint" the patterns of successful, highly recyclable material interactions including tensile strength, barrier performance, carbon rating, and cost-efficiency. This structural logic is stored as a reusable asset, allowing brands to clone high-performing packaging configurations across new SKUs instantly, eliminating "logic loss" when staff or suppliers change.

How does the Transit Failure & Refusal Forensics Engine (TFFE) work?

+
The TFFE automatically analyses the delta between intended packaging design specs and real-world carrier return data. When a parcel is returned or refused, the engine processes the return logs, compiles an automated customer refusal summary, and pinpoints exactly where packaging failed whether due to structural compression, seal degradation, or humidity drift. It then recommends immediate material adjustments to prevent future return cycles.

How does PackSure AI help with UK EPR compliance?

+
PackSure AI provides instant regulatory clarification by mapping complex UK eco-modulation and Plastic Packaging Tax frameworks (£217.85/tonne) directly onto packaging specs during the design phase. The platform automatically calculates EPR RAG scores, recyclability ratings, and generates audit-ready DEFRA compliance reports converting packaging decisions into verifiable regulatory assets rather than manual compliance burdens.

Can PackSure AI integrate with my existing systems?

+
Yes. PackSure AI is built with a supplier-agnostic integration architecture. Pre-built connectors are available for Shopify, Linnworks, WooCommerce, and major ERP platforms. The plug-and-play API platform enables configuration within days, not months. The DNA Onboarding & API Sync service ensures seamless supply chain mapping, secure node configuration, and calibration for platform access for all firms requiring ERP integration.

What is the NFC/QR Trust Ledger and how does it protect my brand?

+
The Scan-Based Risk & Outcome-Learning Trust Ledger integrates serialised, tamper-evident QR codes and NFC identities directly into packaging designs. As products move through global distribution and are scanned by customers, the platform monitors verification behaviour in real time identifying counterfeit hotspots, detecting grey-market diversion, and linking security performance back to financial health. This gives SME owners the same multi-layered brand protection used by multinational consumer goods giants.
